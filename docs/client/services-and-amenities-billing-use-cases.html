1. Purpose and core principles

Go Havena separates Services and Amenities because they answer different questions and bill different entities.

ConceptBilled toWho paysWho is notified
Services Unit (apartment) Unit billing contact One resident per unit
Amenities Resident (individual) Each resident themselves Each resident who owes a bill
Mental model. Services = obligations for the apartment (water, waste, building internet, assigned parking for the unit). Amenities = shared facilities used by people (pool, gym, meeting room, playground).

All services are created by the Community Admin. Residents do not create services; they may subscribe to optional services or pay bills issued to their unit.

2. Services overview

There are two service types. Both bill the unit, not individual residents.

TypeApplies toBilling startsExample
Automatic All eligible units Next billing cycle after publish Water, gas, waste
Optional Units that opt in After subscription is active Extra parking, premium security

Shared bill lifecycle (both service types)

Bill issued → Notice + push notification → Acknowledgement (optional) → Payment proof uploaded → Admin review → Paid or Rejected → If rejected: resident resubmits proof

4. Automatic services — complete flow

Admin creates service (Automatic) ↓ Service saved → applies to ALL active units ↓ Each month (cron or admin “Generate bills”): FOR each eligible unit: create unit_service_bill (unit_id, amount, due_date) resolve billing_contact_user_id ↓ IF billing contact exists: send push notification (one resident) publish notice with invoice + payment instructions optional: require acknowledgement ELSE: alert admin: “Unit has no billing contact” ↓ Billing contact opens notice / Services → Unit bills ↓ Resident acknowledges (if required) ↓ Resident uploads payment proof ↓ Status → pending_verification ↓ Admin reviews → Approve (paid) or Reject (reason; resubmit allowed) ↓ Next month: new bill for same unit

Bill statuses

StatusMeaning
unpaidBill issued, no proof submitted
acknowledgedResident read the notice
pending_verificationProof uploaded, awaiting admin
paidAdmin approved payment
rejectedAdmin rejected; resident may resubmit
overduePast due date, still unpaid

5. Optional services — complete flow

Optional services use per-unit subscription. After activation, billing follows the same pipeline as automatic services.

Admin creates optional service → appears in “Available services” catalog ↓ Billing contact opens catalog → “Subscribe for my unit” ↓ IF approval required: Request submitted → Admin approves/rejects → subscription active ELSE: Instant subscribe → subscription active ↓ Billing starts next cycle ↓ Only subscribed units receive monthly unit_service_bills ↓ Same notice, acknowledgement, proof, and admin review as automatic services

Other unit residents see: “Ask your unit billing contact to subscribe.”

Billing contact may cancel subscription at end of period; no new bills after end date.

6. Amenities overview

Amenities are always resident-based. Unit infrastructure (units, billing contact) does not apply to amenity billing.

Each amenity is configured independently. An amenity uses one primary charge model, or is free.

7. Amenity billing models

ModelWhen chargedWho paysAdmin configuration
Shared monthly billing Every month Each approved resident Shared billing On, amount per resident, due day
Booking / reservation fee At booking time Resident who booked Shared billing Off, bookable + slots, booking fee > 0
Free Never — Shared billing Off, booking fee = 0
Important. Booking fee and shared monthly billing are separate settings. In most cases, use one or the other — not both. Example: Pool = monthly only; Meeting Room = per booking only; Playground = free.

Monthly amenity flow

Admin enables shared billing on amenity ↓ Each month: admin publishes monthly bill ↓ One amenity_resident_bill per approved resident (user_id) ↓ Each resident gets their own notification ↓ Each resident uploads their own payment proof ↓ Admin approves/rejects per resident ↓ Unpaid bills may block that resident from booking

Per-booking amenity flow

Resident selects date and available slot → books ↓ If booking fee > 0 → payment proof at booking time ↓ Admin approves booking and verifies payment ↓ No monthly bill for this amenity

8. Amenity use-case examples

Amenity Pool — Monthly subscription

Billing modelShared monthly billing
Admin settingsShared billing On · amount per resident · due day · booking fee $0
Resident experienceEach resident receives a monthly bill, pays with proof, admin verifies. Pool may use bookable slots for capacity without an extra per-booking charge.

Amenity Meeting Room — Pay per booking

Billing modelBooking / reservation fee
Admin settingsShared billing Off · status Bookable · time slots enabled · booking fee > $0
Resident experiencePick available slot → book → pay and upload proof at booking time → admin confirms payment.

Free Playground — No charge

Billing modelFree

Standard rejection reasons

  • Amount does not match
  • Incorrect bill / payment
  • Invalid receipt
  • Other (with admin note)

12. Resident and admin screens

Resident (mobile / web)

Services

  • My unit bills (billing contact only)
  • Available services — optional catalog
  • Subscribe / request subscription
  • Unit bill history

Amenities

  • Browse facilities
  • My amenity bills (personal)
  • Book slots (if bookable)
  • My bookings

Admin (web portal)

Keep three separate review queues:

  1. Service bills — unit-based, filter by unit / service / status
  2. Amenity bills — resident-based, filter by resident / amenity / status
  3. Booking payments — per booking, per resident
